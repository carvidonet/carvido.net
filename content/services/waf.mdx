Web based applications on the internet have a simile with a person taking the sun somewhere in a nice beach in Mallorca, Spain.
A connected internet is like the sun, emiting UV radiation directly to the skin.

Your common sense tells you that without sunscreen, a sunburn is very likely to happen. A **Web Application Firewall** acts as this first layer of protection, being the sunscreen for your web page.
A **WAF** can act not only as sunscreen but as an umbrella, as a hat or as another sort of protection.

# Blocking the perimeter

Sun radiation, or our simile, DDoS attacks, bad bots, well know vulnerabilities or cyberattacks should be blocked from web sites.
Is there a way to create a safe distance between the wide internet and your application and data?
Deploying a **WAF** is the best way to create a security perimeter. This resource will split your exposed application from your internal infrastructure and data.
