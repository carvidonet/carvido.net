Web based applications on the internet have a simile with a person taking the sun somewhere in a nice beach in Mallorca, Spain.
A connected internet is like the sun, emiting UV radiation directly to the skin.

Your common sense tells you that without sunscreen, a sunburn is very likely to happen. A **Web Application Firewall** acts as this first layer of protection, being the sunscreen for your web page.
A **WAF** can act not only as sunscreen but as an umbrella, as a hat or as another sort of protection.

# Blocking the perimeter

Sun radiation, or our simile, DDoS attacks, bad bots, well know vulnerabilities or cyberattacks should be blocked from web sites.
Is there a way to create a safe distance between the wide internet and your application and data?
Deploying a **WAF** is the way to create a security perimeter. This resource will split your exposed application from your internal infrastructure and data.

# Best in class security

15 SPF, 30 SPF, 50 SPF, water resistant, UVA/UVB, sensitive skin ... You need to know your sunscreen as you need to know the value of a breach in your business.
The right **WAF** solution will provide you with the required defense independently to your application. Best solutions must include protecion no matter where your application reside.

# Always available

Some people doesn't like to apply sunscreen regularly. This is why some clothes include SPF protection as well. Ease your burden and choose a managed cloud-based service.
One of the benefits of a managed **WAF** service is the reduction of risk, misconfigurations, tuning or handling outages. This will allow you to focus more on your business. In addition, a managed service will scale as your business scale, no need to worry about growing funnels.
